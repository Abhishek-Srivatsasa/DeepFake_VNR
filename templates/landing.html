About the Project

Truth. Exposed.
Instantly.

"Pratyaksha" (प्रत्यक्ष) means direct perception in Sanskrit — manifest, self-evident truth. In an era of generative AI and synthetic media, that truth needs defending.

Our system runs entirely on-device. No cloud upload. No account. Just raw detection power built from the ground up — custom training pipeline, balanced dataset, and a dual-model architecture.

Built for a generation that refuses to be deceived.
